⚡ THE 3 CONTENT RULES FOR YOUR BRAND

  1. Make yourself laugh first. If YOU find it funny, your audience will too. Authenticity is everything.

📅 WEEKLY SCHEDULE
  • Mon: Rest. Focus on school. Watch other creators for inspiration.
  • Tue (after school): Edit weekend footage. Create thumbnails.
  • Wed: Upload long video at 2PM. Reply to comments in evening.
  • Thu: Upload second video OR a Short. Engage with community.
  • Fri: Post a Short. Record gameplay for the weekend.
  • Sat: BIG recording day. Film 2–3 video ideas. Post one.
  • Sun: Plan next week's content. Write video ideas. Light editing.
⏰ OPTIMAL UPLOAD TIMES
  • Weekday videos: Schedule at 2–3PM (your local time)
  • Shorts: 3–5PM works great for your age group audience
  • Weekend videos: 10AM–12PM (audience is up earlier on weekends)
  • Avoid: Before 8AM or after 8PM any day
  • Holiday/break periods: Post MORE — your audience has all day to watch
  • Summer: Shift to earlier times (kids awake all day)

🏆 THE GOLDEN CONSISTENCY RULE

  1. Never miss your posting day. The algorithm rewards predictability. Even a short, rough video beats no video.
  2. Batch record on weekends. Spend Saturday filming so you have content ready all week.
  3. Use YouTube's scheduling tool. Set your videos to auto-publish at the right time, even when you're in school.
  4. If you're sick or overwhelmed, post a Short. A 30-second Short keeps momentum without big effort.

Join r/Minecraft (10M+ members) and engage genuinely — share clips when it fits. Join Minecraft Discord servers. Start your own server later. Reddit upvotes on cool clips = thousands of views for free.

⚡ THE REPURPOSING FORMULA

  1. Record 45-min gameplay session (Saturday)
  2. Edit into 10-min YouTube video (Tuesday) → post Wednesday
  3. Clip 3 best moments → 3 Shorts for YouTube/TikTok/Reels (Friday)
  4. Screenshot the funniest frame → post on Instagram with caption
  5. Share clip to Discord/Reddit if it's genuinely funny or impressive
  6. Total: 1 recording → 7+ content pieces across 4 platforms
